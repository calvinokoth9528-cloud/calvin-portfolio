About

Data Science for Public Health

My mission and background.

I'm a Data Scientist and ML Engineer working with the Kenya Medical Research Institute (KEMRI), East Africa's premier health research institution. My work focuses on building data-driven systems for disease surveillance and outbreak prediction.

My flagship project — a KEMRI-inspired malaria outbreak prediction system — integrates climate data from NASA POWER, health indicators from WHO GHO, and socioeconomic data from the World Bank. The system achieved R² = 0.990 using a 7-model XGBoost ensemble with 51 engineered features, forecasting malaria risk 1-2 years ahead with 95% confidence intervals.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Statistics from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), with focus on statistical modeling, machine learning, data visualization, and applied data science. I'm based in Nairobi, Kenya.

I'm passionate about using data science to solve real-world public health challenges in sub-Saharan Africa. Currently accepting senior data science roles, research collaborations, and speaking opportunities. Let's connect.
